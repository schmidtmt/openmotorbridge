# 16 - Legal Compliance, Licenses & Data Privacy (GDPR)

## 1. Automotive Regulatory Approvals & Standards
- **ECE R10 (Electromagnetic Compatibility for Vehicles):** Complies with automotive radiated and conducted emission and immunity limits.
- **RED 2014/53/EU (Radio Equipment Directive):** Applies to the 2.4 GHz Bluetooth subsystem and 868 MHz LoRa transceiver.
- **RoHS 3 (2015/863/EU) & REACH:** Lead-free PCB finishes (ENIG) and eco-compliant PA12 polymer housings.

## 3. Data Protection & Privacy (GDPR / BGH VI ZR 233/17)
- **No Automatic Cloud Upload:** Data is stored strictly on local MicroSD storage unless explicitly synced to the user's private WebDAV instance.
- **Anonymized Ring Buffer:** Unflagged tracking logs are rolled over automatically when storage is low.
